Balance Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

The short version: Balance does not collect, store, sell or share your personal information. The food, workouts and weight you log stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Information we collect

None. Balance has no servers that receive your data, and we cannot see what you log.

How the app uses your device's features

Balance asks for permission before it uses any of these, and you can turn each one off at any time in Settings.

  • Apple Health: to save the food, weight and workouts you log, and to read your steps, active calories, heart rate and weigh-ins. This data moves only between Balance and the Health app on your device. You choose exactly what Balance can read and write in Health › Sharing › Apps › Balance.
  • Apple Watch: to track heart rate and calories during workouts. The watch shares these numbers with your iPhone directly.
  • Camera: to scan food barcodes. Images are never saved or sent anywhere.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode, Balance sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to look up the food's nutrition. No personal information, logs or identifiers are sent with it. Food search uses a database built into the app and works without the internet.

Your data

Everything you log is saved only on your device. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple may include this app data in your device backup under Apple's terms. You can export your data or erase it at any time from the Me tab. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device; anything saved to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in Health.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking tools.

Children

Balance does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
