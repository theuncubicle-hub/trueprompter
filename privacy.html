Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Home Support Privacy Policy
The short version: TruePrompter does not collect, store, sell or share your personal information. Your scripts, recordings and voice stay on your device. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Information we collect

None. TruePrompter has no servers that receive your data, and we cannot see your scripts, videos, audio or anything else you do in the app.

How the app uses your device's features

TruePrompter asks for permission before it uses any of these, and you can turn each one off at any time in Settings › TruePrompter.

  • Camera: to record video in Record mode. Recordings are stored on your device until you save them to Photos or discard them.
  • Microphone: to record audio and to follow your voice so the script scrolls as you speak.
  • Speech recognition: to match your spoken words to your script and to make captions. TruePrompter asks your iPhone to do this on the device. On devices that can't do this on their own, Apple's speech service may process the audio under Apple's Privacy Policy; we never receive it.
  • Photos (add only): to save finished videos to your Photos library when you tap Save. Videos you choose to edit or add are opened only with your selection.
  • Local network: to let another phone or computer on your Wi-Fi act as a remote control. This connection stays on your local network and is protected by a private code shown in the app.
  • Apple Watch and Bluetooth: to receive remote-control button presses. Only commands like play, pause and rewind are sent.
  • AI Magic Script: uses Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence) to write or rewrite scripts. Your script is processed on your device.

Your scripts and settings

Scripts, caption styles and settings are saved only on your device. Files you import (such as Word or PDF documents) are read on your device to copy the text into a script. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple may include this app data in your device backup under Apple's terms.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, and we do not use third-party advertising or tracking tools.

Children

TruePrompter does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

You can delete scripts in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Videos you saved to Photos stay in Photos until you delete them there.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
