Common questions

The floating script doesn't appear over my camera app.

Make sure Picture in Picture is on: Settings › General › Picture in Picture › Start PiP Automatically. Then open TruePrompter, tap Float, and switch to your camera app.

The script isn't following my voice.

Check that TruePrompter can use the Microphone and Speech Recognition in Settings › TruePrompter. Speak at a normal pace; it catches up if you skip a line.

My video didn't save to Photos.

Videos are saved only when you tap Save in the editor. If nothing saves, allow TruePrompter to add to Photos in Settings › TruePrompter › Photos.

How do I import a script?

Tap your script name on the home screen, then Import. Word (.docx), PDF, text and RTF files work. For a Pages or older .doc file, export it as .docx or PDF first.

Captions didn't come out right.

In the editor, open Captions to fix any word, change the style or color, or tap Listen again. Captions follow your cuts automatically.

The Apple Watch remote doesn't show up.

On your iPhone, open the Watch app, scroll to Available Apps, and tap Install next to TruePrompter.

Is there a subscription?

No. TruePrompter is a one-time purchase. There are no subscriptions, ads or accounts.
